04 — CASE STUDIES

Start to finish.

THE PROBLEM

An electric store needed one system to record every sale and keep track of stock, instead of manual record-keeping.

WHAT I BUILT

  • A point of sale: search products, apply a percent or peso discount, charge with 10% tax.
  • Inventory with critical and low-stock alerts.
  • A dashboard with daily, weekly and monthly sales and best sellers.
  • An admin panel, an audit log and PDF reports.

THE RESULT

Stock counts stay accurate, manual record-keeping is reduced, and sales and stock can be seen at a glance.

My part: I handled the whole process — understanding what the store needed, designing the database, building the features and generating the reports.

THE PROBLEM

Staff and teachers needed one place to send HR their leave, payroll, benefits and employment-record requests, and HR needed one place to follow them up.

WHAT I BUILT

  • Requesters pick a category and send a ticket. The leave form counts the days and warns when it is over their balance, which then needs HR Head approval.
  • Each ticket is a conversation with a due date, priority, status and notifications.
  • An HR overview with status filters, internal notes, assignment and an overdue view.
  • Leave balances with adjustment history, reports with CSV export, team management and an audit log.

THE RESULT

HR can submit, track and resolve concerns in one place, and late tickets show in red so the most urgent are handled first.

My part: I was the developer. I built the ticket and leave flow, the reports and the audit log.

THE PROBLEM

A school library needed to run borrowing, visitor attendance, new orders and shelf checks in one system, for a catalog of thousands of titles.

WHAT I BUILT

  • A circulation desk to check out, return and renew by scanning a patron ID and an accession number.
  • Catalog search by title, author, call number or ISBN, with filters and CSV export.
  • Modules for attendance, acquisition, inventory, files, serials and reservations.
  • Printable reports and settings for borrowing rules by patron type.

THE RESULT

Staff run circulation, visits, orders and shelf audits from one system, and print reports or export them.

My part: I was the developer. I built the circulation desk, the modules and the reports.

THE PROBLEM

I wanted a quick game two friends could play together on a phone or a computer.

WHAT I BUILT

  • A start screen to enter your name and pick one of ten colors.
  • A share link and QR code so a friend can join.
  • A game board with scores, new rounds, reset scores and records.
  • Play by clicking a column or pressing the key above it.

THE RESULT

A working two-player game that plays the same on mobile and PC.

My part: I designed the game logic and a simple, responsive interface.
