01 — ABOUT

Turning everyday operations
into working software.

I'm Melecio Laurente, an Information Technology student who learns by building. I like systems I can actually watch work — a sale going through, stock counting itself down, a request moving from open to resolved. I care about clean interfaces, but I care more about logic that doesn't break under real use.

So far I've built ElectroPOS, a point-of-sale and inventory system for an electric store; an HR Ticket System that tracks employment, payroll and leave requests; a School Library System covering circulation, reservations and reports; and Connect4, a game that runs on both mobile and PC. Alongside that, I've earned Cisco Networking Academy badges in networking and HTML.

I'm looking for an on-the-job training or internship placement where I can keep building real systems and learn from a team that ships them.
